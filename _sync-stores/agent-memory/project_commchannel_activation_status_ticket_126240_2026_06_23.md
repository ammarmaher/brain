**SCOPE (user, 2026-06-23):** this CR applies to **communication channels ONLY — NOT applications**, even though both share the "CommChannels & Services" screens. (Contrast sibling 125871 which the user earlier scoped to BOTH kinds; 126240 is channels-only.)

**Core change:** CommChannel no longer becomes Active immediately on payment. Payment → new intermediate status **"Paid"**; Active only when **Paid AND ≥1 associated Service Account is Active**. Affects two FE areas: Org-Hierarchy → CommChannels&Services tab, and CommChannels & Services Management.

126240 asks for: new list columns (Visibility, Name, Price type, Price value, **Purchase date** NEW, **First Activation Date**, **Paid date** = renamed from "Activation date", Renew date, Status, Actions); new action **Activate** (shown only when status=Paid → navigates to that channel's Service Account page); **Disable/Enable actions REMOVED**; statuses = Inactive(first)/**Paid(NEW)**/Active/Expired/InActive/Disabled; **lock visibility editing once purchased**; on making a channel visible, warn if tenant has ≥1 active contract ("Make sure to edit all active contracts and add the contract price details and Rate card…").

**KEY CODE-GAP FINDINGS (grounded):** [CODE] `Falcon.Commerce.Domain/Constants/Enums .cs` `eFalconServiceStatus` = None0/InActive1/Active2/Expired3/Disabled4 — **NO "Paid"**; `eFalconServiceAction` = DoPayment1/Disable2/Enable3/EditPriceType4/EditPriceValue5 — **NO "Activate"**. [CODE] `FalconServiceConfigurationBase.Operations.cs` `Activate()` sets FirstActivationDate/ActivationDate/Status=Active/Renew() with **no Service-Account check**; payment path = `ValidateActivationEligibility()`→`Activate()` (payment directly activates today). **"Service Account" business entity does NOT exist in code** (only Zitadel machine-account in deprecated identity-svc). So this ticket introduces a new status, a new action, and depends on a new/parallel Service-Account concept. Directly continues the [[project_hide_active_service_status_reset_balance_bug_2026_06_22]] visibility⟂status work (BR-AM-40 Hide-while-Active).

**BUSINESS ANSWERS RECEIVED (2026-06-24, via filled answers HTML — author-authoritative):** (Q1, YES) **Service Account DEFINED**: Falcon user makes channel visible→client `DoPayment`→success⇒status **Paid**⇒Paid lets the **client create Service Account(s) for that channel** (on a NEW "Service Account page")⇒once **≥1 Service Account is Active**, channel⇒**Active** ("ready for use"). Some channels have **approval/review flows** for service accounts (→ SA not instantly active). The 9 visibility×status scenarios were pasted by the business = the transition-rule SoT (largely supersedes the 401 Google Sheet). (Q2) **No auto-refund** (confirmed; matches Charging preserve-only); a **Paid channel's expiration/renewal is driven by its PRICING TYPE** (so Paid is NOT indefinite — it can expire/renew per Monthly/Yearly/OneTime even before activation); stuck-Paid handled **operationally** (CS+PM). (Q3) During **Paid** the client may **create templates + prepare/fill contracts (+ wallet top-up)** to accelerate onboarding; RESIDUAL CONTRADICTION: the Q3 checkbox says "all incl. send" = Yes, but Q1 ("ready for use" only when Active) + Scenario 3 (send tied to Active) imply **send requires Active** — recommend send-gated-on-Active, flag to confirm. (Q4) **Visibility lock**: once a channel is **paid & first-activated**, it can **NEVER be hidden again; NO role override**. (Q5) Reveal warning = **advisory popup** (non-blocking) when making visible while active contracts exist; **NEW HARD GATE**: the SA page's **"Create Service Account" button is DISABLED until contract price details are filled in ALL active contracts** for that tenant/channel; **"active contract" = expiration date not yet passed**. (Q6) After renewal→Active, **sending IS allowed** (resolves the Scenario-9 "Hide in Use" slip). NET: Service Account most plausibly = the Provisioning `CommunicationChannelService`/WABA scaffolding (client-created, per-channel, approval flow) → recommended architecture = **HYBRID**: Commerce stays status/display/payment SoR consuming a "SA-active" signal; Provisioning owns the Service-Account entity/page/lifecycle. Updated understanding ~98% (residuals = Commerce-vs-Provisioning SoR decision + the 1 send-during-Paid contradiction + whether the SA page/feature is in THIS CR or a prerequisite ticket).
