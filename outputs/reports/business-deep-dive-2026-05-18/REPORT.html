📊 Executive Summary

What this report covers and the most important shifts revealed by the mining pass.

The headlines

  • 221 business rules exist across 5 modules. 41 are OPEN (PRD-silent or contradictory). Until they close, the platform behavior on those topics depends on whoever ships the code first.
  • Module 05 Templates is the largest knowledge hole. Only ~12% of the 982-line source PRD made it into the vault. Voice + AI template flows are entirely missing. The Template entity has zero backend DTOs and no E-template.md vault note.
  • 26 V-rules cover only ~25% of the 221 BR rules. Wallet topology (BR-AM-20..38), contract lifecycle (BR-CC-10..14), template wizard (BR-TM-02..16) are essentially unvalidated.
  • 16 E-* entities in vault; 34 more are referenced by BR rules but undefined. The single biggest missing entity is E-permission-group — cited everywhere, modeled nowhere.
  • Cross-module workflows are documented only for Add Client. Send Transaction (the most important platform flow), Add Contract, Transfer Balance, Do Payment, First Login — none have an A→Z trace.
  • 105 yes/no questions need product-team answers. Tick the answers below; the report auto-saves and you can export the full set as JSON to share back.
