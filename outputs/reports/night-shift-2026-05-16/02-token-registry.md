## Dark-theme overrides

Two dark-mode override sites exist:

1. **`libs/falcon-theme/src/falcon-tailwind-tokens.css`** - re-declares `--color-falcon-neutral-*`, teal alphas, and shadow tokens under `:where(.app-dark, .app-dark *), :where(.dark, .dark *)`. SSOT-level cascade so every consumer of those raw color tokens auto-inverts.
