# Prerequisites to App Development

Throughout these instructions, "you" refers to the person(s) performing the actions. This may involve multiple individuals, such as:

- A business administrator setting up the credit line and business portfolio
- A developer configuring the application

For simplicity, it is recommended that one person completes all the steps if possible.

To set up your organization and business portfolio:

1. **Setup a Credit Line:** Contact your Meta representative to create an organization with an extended credit line from Meta.

2. **Set Up Business Portfolio:** Create a Business Portfolio in MBS. Complete steps within Meta Business Suite using Admin level access on the Business Portfolio.

3. **Set Up Application:** Create an application owned by the created Business Portfolio.
