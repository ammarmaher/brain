# Business Creative Asset Management

This API allows you to create folders to store and share ad creatives between your business and another. Advertisers own business creative and assets in the folders. You send sharing requests to business admins in Business Manager and email notifications to business admins for their approval.

This API is under limited availability. Please contact your Facebook representative for access
