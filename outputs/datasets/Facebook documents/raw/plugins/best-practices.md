# Best Practices for Social Plugins

You can use the Facebook social plugins to integrate features such as comments, like, and sharing. For more information, see [Social Plugins](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/plugins).

Use the best practices in this documentation to improve the experience for your users.

## Checklists

Follow all the [Developer Guidelines for Social Plugins](https://developers.facebook.com/devpolicy#socialplugins), including the following:

- Don't include or pair Social Plugins or Login with non-Meta ads.
- Don't sell or purchase placement of social plugins or sharer.php.
- Don't obscure or cover elements of social plugins.
