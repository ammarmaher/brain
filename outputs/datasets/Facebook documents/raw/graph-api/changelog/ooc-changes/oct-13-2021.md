# October 13, 2021

## Reach Estimate

We understand that the shift to ranges may be challenging for some to implement by October 27, 2021. As such, through November 15, we will continue to make available a single-point estimate as an additional endpoint.

The following changes apply to _potential reach_ for all versions:

- The name of _potential reach_ is changing to _estimated audience size_. Estimated audience size generally estimates how many people meet the targeting and ad placement criteria that advertisers select while creating an ad. [Learn more about reach estimate](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/reference/reach-estimate/).

- The definition of the [`reachestimate` API](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/audiences/guides/reach-estimate) has changed from _Get an estimated number of individuals for your targeting and what bids to make to reach that target group with the /reachestimate API_ to _Get a range of individuals for your targeting and what bids to make to reach that target group with the /reachestimate API_.

- The [`GET /{act_ad-account-id}/reachestimate`](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/audiences/guides/reach-estimate) endpoint has two new fields: `users_lower_bound` and `users_upper_bound`.

- On October 27, 2021, the `users` field will be deprecated.


## Delivery Estimate

The following changes apply to _delivery estimate_ for all versions:

- The [`GET /{adset-id}/delivery_estimate`](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/reference/ad-campaign-delivery-estimate/) endpoint has two new fields: `estimate_mau_lower_bound` and `estimate_mau_upper_bound`.

- The [`GET /{ad-account-id}/delivery_estimate`](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/reference/ad-campaign-delivery-estimate/) endpoint has two new fields: `estimate_mau_lower_bound` and `estimate_mau_upper_bound`.

- The [`GET /{ad-account-id}/adsets`](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/reference/ad-account/adsets/) endpoint has two new fields: `estimate_mau_lower_bound` and `estimate_mau_upper_bound`.

- On October 27, 2021, the `estimate_mau` field will be deprecated.


## Reach & Frequency

The following changes apply to reach & frequency for all versions and only applied for predictions created after October 29, 2021 onward. Old predictions won't show any size after November 15, 2021.

- The [`GET /{act_ad-account-id}/reachfrequencypredictions`](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/reference/reach-frequency-prediction/) endpoint has two new fields: `audience_size_lower_bound` and `audience_size_upper_bound`.

- The [`GET /{act_ad-account-id}/ReachFrequencyPrediction`](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/reference/reach-frequency-prediction/) endpoint has two new fields: `audience_size_lower_bound` and `audience_size_upper_bound`.

- On October 27, 2021, the `target_audience_size` field will be deprecated.


## Detailed Targeting

The following changes apply to _detailed targeting_ for all versions:

- The [`GET /{act_ad-account-id}/targetingsearch`](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/audiences/reference/targeting-search/) endpoint has two new fields: `audience_size_lower_bound` and `audience_size_upper_bound`.

- The [`GET /{act_ad-account-id}/targetingbrowse`](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/audiences/reference/detailed-targeting/#browse) endpoint has two new fields: `audience_size_lower_bound` and `audience_size_upper_bound`.

- The [`GET /{act_ad-account-id}/targetingsuggestions`](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/audiences/reference/detailed-targeting/#suggestions) has two new fields: `audience_size_lower_bound` and `audience_size_upper_bound`.

- The [`GET /{act_ad-account-id}/targetingvalidation`](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/audiences/reference/detailed-targeting/#validation) has two new fields: `audience_size_lower_bound` and `audience_size_upper_bound`.

- On October 27, 2021, the `audience_size` field will be deprecated.


## Custom Broad Category Targeting

The following changes apply to _custom broad category targeting_ for all versions:

- The [`GET /{act_ad-account-id}/broadtargetingcategories`](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/audiences/reference/advanced-targeting) endpoint has two new fields: `size_lower_bound` and `size_upper_bound`. See also [Custom Broad Category Targeting, example](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/audiences/reference/advanced-targeting#broadcategories).

- On October 27, 2021, the `size` field will be deprecated.


## Custom Audience

The following changes apply to _custom audience_ for all versions:

- The [`GET /search/customaudience`](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/reference/custom-audience) endpoint has two new fields: `approximate_count_lower_bound` and \`approximate\_count\_upper\_bound.
- The [`GET /{act_ad-account-id}/customaudiences`](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/reference/custom-audience) endpoint has two new fields: `approximate_count_lower_bound` and `approximate_count_upper_bound`.

- The [`GET /savedaudience`](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/reference/saved-audience/) endpoint has two new fields: `approximate_count_lower_bound` and `approximate_count_upper_bound`.

- On October 27, 2021, the `approximate_count` field will be deprecated.


## Search

The following change applies to _search_ for all versions:

- The [`GET /search/adinterest`](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/audiences/reference/targeting-search#interests) endpoint has two new fields: `audience_size_lower_bound` and `audience_size_upper_bound`.

- On October 27, 2021, the `audience_size` field will be deprecated.


## Learn More

For more details, see the Facebook for [Business blog](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/update-to-our-pre-campaign-estimates) and the [About Estimated Audience Size, Business Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1665333080167380?id=176276233019487).
