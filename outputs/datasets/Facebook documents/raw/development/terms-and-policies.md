# Terms and Policies

You must comply with our [Platform Terms](https://developers.facebook.com/terms) and [Developer Policies](https://developers.facebook.com/devpolicy) to remain active on our platform, so please take the time to carefully read them both.

## Enforcement

If you violate any of our terms or policies your app and possibly your account will be enforced upon. Refer to our [Enforcement](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/development/terms-and-policies/enforcement) document to learn about our enforcement protocols and to ensure we can reach you if we detect a violation from one of your apps.
