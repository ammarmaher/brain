# Meta Content Library

[Meta Content Library](https://www.facebook.com/transparency-tools/content-library) is a web-based tool that allows researchers to explore and understand data across Meta technologies by offering a comprehensive, visual, and searchable collection of publicly accessible content.

See [Meta Content Library API](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/content-library-and-api/content-library-api) for information about the complementary API product that allows researchers to programmatically pull data from the same public content library.

For product support, refer to [Support](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/content-library-and-api/support).

For information about ongoing issues, visit the [Data Transparency Status page](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fmetastatus.com%2Fdata-transparency&h=AUAqO0uCAMORx1SMB5iJrrx98rTa3oXfdS9-QJ_nHEF3-u3N1DSxqj7w1DibuV3PPCHWBMNSYiIFi92nW4zPGZNPeQfQaH4c1D85HDKsKZ37ISSgQj5KCNvInisE-JltAVSrH4VvP23O5A).

## Scope of included data

### Facebook

#### Public content dataset

Posts to the following:

- Pages, groups and events
- Profiles with 100 or more followers
- Profiles that are [verified](https://www.facebook.com/help/196050490547892)

Meta Content Library surfaces posts by original producers only (excludes collaborators).

#### Downloadable subset

Posts to the above surfaces from the following:

- Pages with 15,000 or more likes or followers
- Profiles with 25,000 or more followers
- Profiles that are [verified](https://www.facebook.com/help/196050490547892)

**Note**: Downloads are only available if the Inter-university Consortium for Political and Social Research (ICPSR) or Secure Data Access Center (CASD) has approved your Meta Content Library application and you have consented to the [Meta Research Tools Terms and Conditions](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Ftransparency.meta.com%2Fresearchtools%2Fproduct-terms-meta-research&h=AUAtu4LKzTRGtSZsz2g_ORBdvWQTXlDJPXMGf6jOS9Yp_qNRYUuf68bVGBl8uSoi_mA0Fi7SDXL1TVtE8nj9xH0iuFHD4C7So_A2tY0HfObUpW-ryV81nLrtqdzAYBJ4vh7MSXOFvbgFGg).

### Facebook Marketplace

#### Public content dataset

Public listings on Facebook Marketplace from Pages or profiles, excluding the following:

- Listings marked as sold more than 1 month ago.
- Listings that have not been updated in the last 6 months.
- Listings with a privacy setting enabled, such as hiding a listing from friends.
- Job listings in some locations. See [About jobs on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/help/2203257093390031) for more information.

### Facebook fundraisers

#### Public content dataset

- Public fundraisers on Facebook
- Fundraisers attached to public posts
- Reshares of public fundraisers (available in the posts data subset)

### Facebook channels

#### Public content dataset:

- Active channels with audience set to “anyone” or “followers” as long as the creator of the channel meets the criteria for inclusion in the Facebook public content dataset.
- All channel messages within public channels.

### Instagram

#### Public content dataset

Posts from the following:

- Business and creator accounts
- Personal accounts with 100 or more followers
- Personal accounts that are [verified](https://www.facebook.com/help/196050490547892)

#### Downloadable subset

Posts from the following:

- Business and creator accounts with 25,000 or more followers
- Personal accounts with 25,000 or more followers
- Business, creator and personal accounts that are [verified](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F733907830039577%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUA5ArZod2XTT-dMEHP5oNYhQAj-4OVqMQs16wkHIX8HAOM9POS4MUN0guKyZebFf6kS8xBJqDFCLcHS2p2AXNhPYbLxKhQiV-L5S_2nORQoNFVi-yiLVj-TVXDWSnReTcl3x7lhKxNUqw)

Meta Content Library surfaces posts by original producers only (excludes collaborators).

**Note**: Downloads are only available if the Inter-university Consortium for Political and Social Research (ICPSR) or Secure Data Access Center (CASD) has approved your Meta Content Library application and you have consented to the [Meta Research Tools Terms and Conditions](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Ftransparency.meta.com%2Fresearchtools%2Fproduct-terms-meta-research&h=AUAMFzejpDwXKnWeK9t5LZBtujHMj1GcbQ0jL4oTC8KpYoun18pZU29vR_wdQQ8XunhlAdWjQreIkqiwo0WK2QQsH7Fm4bAOoICgMgpgREYyqn-RSs3mZuyTNX3hGajXANTvvb5Dm3HYOEC5WTtMMa_ISHw).

### Instagram fundraisers

#### Public content dataset:

- Fundraisers created by public Instagram accounts

- Reshares of fundraisers (available in the posts data subset)


### Instagram channels

#### Public content dataset:

- Active channels with audience set to “anyone” or “followers” as long as the creator of the channel meets the criteria for inclusion in the Instagram public content dataset.

- All channel messages within public channels.


### Threads

#### Public content dataset:

- Posts shared by public profiles with 100 or more followers

**Note**: Threads content is not available for download while this dataset is in development as data quality may have significant variation.

### WhatsApp channels

#### Public content dataset:

- WhatsApp channels that are verified or have a minimum of 100 followers.

- All channel updates within eligible channels from the last 30 days.


## Geographic scope

In scope: Public data from public Facebook Pages, groups, events and profiles, from public Instagram accounts and channels, from Threads profiles, and from WhatsApp channels from most countries/territories. No public data/content is available from the following countries:

- China
- North Korea
- South Korea
- Togo

This means that:

1. At least one admin of a public Facebook Page, group, event or profile, a public Instagram account or channel, a Threads profile or WhatsApp channel must have a stated location that is in a non-excluded country or region for information about that Page, group, event, profile, account or channel to be accessible. The owner of a public Instagram account or Threads profile must have a primary country or region that is in a non-excluded country or region for information about that account or profile to be accessible.

2. Of the Facebook Pages, groups, events and profiles, Instagram accounts and channels, Threads profiles and WhatsApp channels that qualify based on #1, only posts or messages made by users in non-excluded countries/territories are in scope.


Multimedia content from the following states in the United States will be excluded from data downloads (downloads not available for all data types):

- Illinois
- Texas

### For Facebook Marketplace listings

In scope:

- Listings made by profiles are surfaced in Content Library as long as the profile is in a non-excluded country.

- Listings made by public Pages are surfaced in Content Library as long as at least one Page admin is in a non-excluded country.


### For Facebook fundraisers

In scope: Fundraisers from public Facebook profiles and nonprofits (registered and onboarded with Meta's fundraising tools) from countries where Facebook fundraisers are available.

### For Instagram fundraisers

In scope: Fundraisers from public instagram accounts and nonprofits (registered and onboarded with Meta's fundraising tools) from countries where Instagram fundraisers are available.

## Audience-restricted content

Facebook and Instagram content can have age-based or location-based audience restrictions. This can affect the data surfaced in Meta Content Library user interface (UI) and API as follows:

### Age restrictions

Age restrictions may be imposed by producers on their content, or restrictions can be based on the classification of content as less appropriate for certain age groups.

Meta Content Library UI and API may not surface age-restricted content as it is not considered publicly accessible.

### Location restrictions

Location restrictions may be imposed for a variety of reasons, such as producers limiting visibility of their content, local laws, legal actions, etc. For example, see [Content Restrictions Based on Local Law](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Ftransparency.meta.com%2Freports%2Fcontent-restrictions%2F&h=AUC-fHeVr6IplCOBLbEe28EfOzh7UaWhwHTtSvmSPkRZ2wrW8UOaVsR1B2shl1Ksgq9f_cDVwt5h4TeA-Uj_jDr6BLoAh99EsiiveXrXNlQj9SDeV6bMkJsQoHWq-HmCPpu7wGaeVND3Og).

#### Facebook

- Meta Content Library UI does not surface location-restricted content to users accessing the tool in that restricted location.

- Location-restricted content is unavailable to users accessing Meta Content Library API using a cleanroom that is in a restricted location.


#### Instagram

- Location-restricted content is not publicly accessible and will not be surfaced in Meta Content Library UI or API.

#### WhatsApp

- Meta Content Library UI does not surface location-restricted content to users accessing the tool in that restricted location.

- Location-restricted content is not surfaced to users accessing Meta Content Library API using a cleanroom that is in a restricted location.


## Language scope

Search results in all languages are included unless the search is filtered based on language (language filter is not available for all types of data).

## Components of a Facebook post

- Post reactions: Number of reactions on the post.

- Post comments: Number of comments on the post. Comments on the post can be accessed by clicking the **View comments** link. This includes comments from all producers who responded to the post. A maximum of 50 comments are loaded on the comments page at a time. To load the next 50 comments, click **Load more** at the bottom of the page. Within the loaded comments you can:

  - Search for text
  - Sort
  - Specify a time and date range

- Post shares: Number of shares of the post.

- Post views: Number of times the post was on screen, not including times it appeared on the post owner’s screen. For video posts, views are counted whether the video was played or not. Only posts with more than 100 views display the view count. A post displays no view count value if there were fewer than 100 views as of the last refresh. View counts for posts created within the last 180 days are refreshed approximately every 24 hours, provided the post has accumulated more than 10 views within that 24 hour period. If not, view counts are refreshed every 5-7 days. View counts for posts created more than 180 days ago are refreshed every 5-7 days. View counts are not available for posts created before January 1, 2017. Views of content may register differently on Instagram and Facebook: On Facebook, a content view is logged when content is either fully visible or covers at least 50% of the screen height for 250ms. On Instagram, the content needs to be at least 50% visible for the same duration to register a content view.

- Post owner: Original producer of the post.

- Post activity type: Type of activity represented in the post. Activity types include streaming or playing (of a gaming video).

- Post activity name: Name of the activity represented in the post. For example, if the activity type is streaming (of a gaming video), this would be the name of the game being played.

- Post surface country (for posts from Pages and profiles only): Primary country or region of the Page admin(s) of the post, or primary country or region of the profile owner of the post.

- Post date: Date posted to Facebook.

- Post media (text): Full text shared in the post.

- Post media (image): Images shared in the post.

- Post media (video and reels): Videos and reels shared in the post.

- Post media (link): Complete URL of the link shared in the post.


## Components of a Facebook Marketplace listing

- Listing views: Number of times the listing was on screen, not including times it appeared on the seller’s screen. View counts are refreshed every 2-3 days. Listings with no views as of the last refresh display “N/A” for the view count.

- Listing date: Date listed on Facebook Marketplace.

- Listing title: Title of the listing.

- Price: Price and currency shared in the listing.

- Listing location: Location shared in the listing.

- Listing media (image): Images shared in the listing.

- Listing media (video): Video shared in the listing.

- Description: Seller’s description of the listing (click **View details** to access).


## Components of a Facebook fundraiser

- Donors: Current number of donors contributing to the fundraiser.

- Invited: Number of people invited to the fundraiser.

- Shares: Number of times the fundraiser has been shared.

- Organizer: Organizer of the fundraiser. Shown if the organizer meets eligibility criteria. For profiles that do not meet eligibility criteria, the organizer’s username will display as “Facebook profile."

- Beneficiary: Name of the nonprofit or person the fundraiser benefits.

- Date/time: Date and time the fundraiser was created.

- Title: Title of the fundraiser.

- Progress toward goal: Amount of money raised by the fundraiser to date and the goal amount, in the currency of the fundraiser. A green bar indicates the fundraiser has met or exceeded its goal. A blue bar shows the progress toward the goal.

- View details: Click to access additional information about the fundraiser including, activity and updates, and details about the beneficiary.


## Components of a Facebook channel search results list

The results of a Facebook channel search are displayed in tabular format with the following information available for each channel:

- Channel name: Name of the channel.
- Description: The description for the channel.
- Members: Number of channel members.
- Date created: Date the channel was created.
- View activity button: Click to view the channel activity page.

## Components of a Facebook channel activity page

### Search loaded messages field

- Enter text to search within the first 50 loaded messages (50 messages are loaded at a time).
- To search in the next set of 50, click Load more at the bottom of the channel activity page.

### Message cards

- Reactions: Number of reactions on the message. Hover over the information icon for a breakdown of the top five reactions.
- Producer name: Name of the message producer. Hover over the name to display the producer’s description and number of followers.
- Date and time: Date and time the message was sent.
- Message content: Content of the message (text, photo, video, audio, shared Facebook post or link, poll, prompt).

### About the channel

- Name of the channel
- Number of members of the channel
- Channel creation date
- Approximate number of messages on the channel

### Admin

- Name and username of the channel admin.

## Components of an Instagram post

- Post likes: Number of like reactions on the post.

- Post comments: Number of comments on the post. Comments on the post can be accessed by clicking the **View comments** link. This includes comments from all producers who responded to the post. A maximum of 50 comments are loaded on the comments page at a time. To load the next 50 comments, click **Load more** at the bottom of the page. Within the loaded comments you can:

  - Search for text
  - Sort
  - Specify a time and date range

- Post views: Number of times the post was on screen, not including times it appeared on the post owner’s screen. For video posts, views are counted whether the video was played or not. Only posts with more than 100 views display the view count. A post displays no view count value if there were fewer than 100 views as of the last refresh. View counts for posts created within the last 180 days are refreshed approximately every 24 hours, provided the post has accumulated more than 10 views within that 24 hour period. If not, view counts are refreshed every 3-5 days. View counts for posts created more than 180 days ago are refreshed every 3-5 days. View counts are not available for posts made before October 1, 2022. Views of content may register differently on Instagram and Facebook: On Facebook, a content view is logged when content is either fully visible or covers at least 50% of the screen height for 250ms. On Instagram, the content needs to be at least 50% visible for the same duration to register a content view. Note that this metric differs from the [Views metric](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F788388387972460&h=AUCeorKs90SMZS9DAht1stpEcwy2bWpgPskc_mMC92BMm6pNnMLaz_Dw4WuIIlWnlLT0bUy2HZLJzPwWcg9sWdY7fsb9YbJmdAsP2PUNQjRY1WVsZ2_C8wnjnsx9KdVZGlz5Cx1QqELC-g) displayed on Instagram, which measures the number of times a reel started to play and the number of times a non-reel displayed on a person’s screen.

- Post owner: Original producer of the post.

- Post date: Date posted to Instagram.

- Post media (image): Images shared in the post.

- Post media (videos and reels): Videos and reels shared in the post.

- Post media (album): Posts containing two or more images or videos.


## Components of an Instagram fundraiser

- Donations: Current number of donations contributed to the fundraiser.

- Organizer: Organizer of the fundraiser. Shown if the organizer meets eligibility criteria. For profiles that do not meet eligibility criteria, the organizer’s username will display as "Instagram account".

- Beneficiary: Name of the nonprofit the fundraiser benefits.

- Date/time: Date and time the fundraiser was created.

- Title: Title of the fundraiser.

- Progress toward goal: Amount of money raised by the fundraiser to date and the goal amount, in the currency of the fundraiser. A green bar indicates the fundraiser has met or exceeded its goal. A blue bar shows the progress toward the goal.

- View details: Click to access additional information about the fundraiser including details about the beneficiary and organizer.


## Components of an Instagram channel search results list

The results of a channel search are displayed in tabular format with the following information available for each channel:

- Channel name: Name of the channel.

- Members: Number of channel members.

- Date created: Date the channel was created.

- View activity button: Click to view the channel activity page.


## Components of an Instagram channel activity page

### Search loaded messages field

- Enter text to search within the first 50 loaded messages (50 messages are loaded at a time).
- To search in the next set of 50, click Load more at the bottom of the channel activity page.

### Message cards

- Reactions: Number of reactions on the message. Hover over the information icon for a breakdown of the top five reactions.
- Producer name: Name of the producer of the message. Hover over the name to display the producer’s description and number of followers.
- Date and time: Date and time the message was sent.
- Message content: Content of the message (text, photos, video, audio, shared Instagram post or link, poll, daily prompt).

### About the channel

- Name of the channel
- Number of members of the channel
- Channel creation date
- Approximate number of messages on the channel
- Channel status as Active or Ended
- Admin
- Name and username of the channel admin.

### Collaborators

- Names and usernames of channel collaborators for collaborators who meet eligibility criteria. Those who do not meet eligibility criteria are listed as “Instagram account,” and only their Meta Content Library ID is shown.

### Moderators

## Components of a Threads post

- Post likes: Number of like reactions on the post.

- Post replies: Number of replies on the post, including replies to previous replies. Replies on the post can be accessed by clicking the **View replies** link. This includes replies from all producers who responded to the post. A maximum of 50 replies are loaded on the replies page at a time. To load the next 50 replies, click **Load more** at the bottom of the page. Within the loaded replies you can:

  - Search for text
  - Sort
  - Specify a time and date range

- Reposts: Number of times the content was reposted.

- Post views: Number of times the post was on screen, not including times it appeared on the post owner’s screen. For original posts, view counts include views of the post itself but do not include views of reposts or quotes of the post. For quotes, view counts include views of the quote but do not include views of the original post or reposts. For replies, view counts include views of the reply but do not include views of the original post, quotes or reposts. For video posts, views are counted whether the video was played or not. Only posts with more than 100 views display the view count. A post displays no view count value if there were fewer than 100 views as of the last refresh. View counts for posts created within the last 180 days are refreshed every 24 hours, provided the post has accumulated more than 10 views within a 24 hour period after September 17, 2024. View counts for posts created more than 180 days ago are refreshed every 2 days. View counts are not available for posts created before March 17, 2024.

- Post owner: Original producer of the post.

- Post date: Date posted to Threads.

- Post media (text-only): Posts that only contain text and no other types of media.

- Post media (photos): Images shared in the post.

- Post media (videos): Videos shared in the post.

- Post media (albums): Posts containing two or more images or videos.

- Post media (links): Posts containing one or more links.

## Additional information

- The search index only searches raw text of content from public Facebook Pages, groups and events, and from public Instagram accounts and public Threads profiles. It does not search metadata of photos, full URL matches, or comments. Occasionally results will match terms that are semantically related to a query term, or contain the query term as a substring. Tags in a post cannot be searched.

- Search is case insensitive and only complete matches are returned (not partial matches).

- The user interface retrieves only the top 1,000 search results, so the search results are not necessarily representative of all Facebook, Instagram or Threads posts on the search term in the specific timeframe of the query.

- The top 1,000 search results can differ slightly day to day, not only due to newly created content but also because post owners might have deleted the content or modified the privacy settings.

- Newly created Facebook profiles, Instagram accounts or Threads profiles, and content posted by these newly created profiles and accounts may not be included in Meta Content Library for up to 4 days.

- Grouping multiple keywords in querying the data is not supported for languages that are read right-to-left.

- Each researcher is limited to a maximum of 60 searches per minute.

- Each researcher is limited to 500,000 total search results for Content Library UI per 7-day rolling window.

- Results in downloaded CSV files from Content Library will be counted towards your 500,000 total search results limit. You also have a separate multimedia query budget in your downloads of 1,000 search results per 7-day rolling window.
