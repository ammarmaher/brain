# Terms and Policies

- [Facebook Cookie Policy](https://www.facebook.com/policies/cookies/) – See our Cookie Policy for details about the cookies used and the data received.
- [General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/facebook-pixel/implementation/gdpr) – It applies to companies (regardless of where they are based) who process personal data about individuals in the European Union.
