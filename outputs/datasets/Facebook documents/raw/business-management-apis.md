# Business Management APIs

Use the Business Management APIs to create and maintain a business' organic and paid presence on Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp —some of these functions can also be performed on Facebook's [Business Manager UI](https://business.facebook.com/).

## Common Use Cases

- [On Behalf Of](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/business-manager/guides/on-behalf-of): Get access to act on behalf of your clients.
- [Manage Your Business' Assets](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/business-asset-management/guides/assets): Get, add, and delete assets from a business.
- [2-Tier Business Manager Solution](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/2tier-bm-solution): Create and manage child businesses.
