# Ad Formats for Audience Network

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ## Banner and medium rectangle ads<br>Banner ads are the most common type of ad unit and can be placed throughout your app. A banner is a small bar ad that appears at the bottom or top of your content. Banner ads usually measure 320 x 50, Medium Rectangle ads usually measure 300 x 250.<br>Learn how to [change placements in Monetization Manager](https://business.facebook.com/pub/property/adspace/placement/settings) from Banner to the Medium Rectangle format if these were previously configured as Banner for bidding.<br>- Avoid placing banner / medium rectangle ads over text, image, and other clickable portions of your app to prevent accidental clicks.<br>- Only include one ad per page or show one ad at a time if scrolling.<br>- Avoid placing banner / medium rectangle ads in an area that interrupts the natural flow of the app to prevent accidental clicks.<br>- Do not edit or modify banner / medium rectangle ads in any way without Facebook's written approval. | ![Shazam](https://scontent-lax3-1.xx.fbcdn.net/v/t39.2178-6/10574689_1462074490723267_486270503_n.png?_nc_cat=104&ccb=1-7&_nc_sid=34156e&_nc_ohc=hIgZpotqw9UQ7kNvwGdnLuJ&_nc_oc=AdrXK6jeOXRaodfYATGbe6gQMtp69sHU1BTNzSfnhbRinlsvSgh7fR9rpgT1eBDS4Vo&_nc_zt=14&_nc_ht=scontent-lax3-1.xx&_nc_gid=1yrBmLAOyQ6XDZLgOkyyMg&_nc_ss=7b289&oh=00_Af6h9hfHeNW6fIM2p48PfrKcInYOWAsotBTwp5h0yMDUbA&oe=6A112B2F) |

| ## Interstitial ads<br>Interstitial ads are full screen ads that can be effective in catching a user's attention. Many publishers recommend interstitial ads for pauses in game play or times when there is a natural break in your app's flow.<br>- Interstitial ads must not be implemented in such a way that may surprise users or interrupt the natural flow of the app. They should be placed in between natural breaks in the experience of your app. For example, interstitial ads should be shown between levels or stages for gaming apps and are not permitted on placements such as app load, exit pages or in the middle of game play. <br>- The call-to-action must be shown in a way that when a user clicks on the ad it is intentional.<br>- Do not edit or modify interstitial ads in any way without Facebook's written approval. | ![Shazam](https://scontent-lax7-1.xx.fbcdn.net/v/t39.2178-6/10574687_1476907822575876_82173515_n.png?_nc_cat=105&ccb=1-7&_nc_sid=34156e&_nc_ohc=7ro6koSCc2UQ7kNvwGNPJRL&_nc_oc=Ado4NLm_LMgDpQ8RgM5hTsrgJqzrtq-hsXbFUZwrxIn7TW1-1E0YyLMyxS0S5KHNSos&_nc_zt=14&_nc_ht=scontent-lax7-1.xx&_nc_gid=1yrBmLAOyQ6XDZLgOkyyMg&_nc_ss=7b289&oh=00_Af63uiPwMI4WVXZTcXBqup7uMZxe39araUCa6t2tTVpUtw&oe=6A110836) |

| ## Native ads<br>Native ads give you the control to design the perfect ad units for your app. With our Native Ad API, you can determine the look and feel, size and location of your ads. Because you decide how the ads are formatted, ads can fit seamlessly in your application.<br>- Clearly mark your native ad as "Sponsored", "Promoted" or "Ad" to distinguish it from the rest of your content AND include the expandable "AdOptionsView" icon on your native ad by configuring the AdOptionsView control as expandable (see [FBAdOptionsVideo](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/audience-network/reference/ios/classes/fbadoptionsview.html) for iOS or [AdOptionsVideo](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/audience-network/reference/android/com/facebook/ads/adoptionsview.html) for Android)<br>- Your native ad should include the advertisers' creative assets in a way where users are encouraged to engage with the ad unit. <br>- You can scale the campaign image, but do not obscure, cover, distort, or otherwise alter the campaign image.<br>- Campaign images should be optimized for images with ratios of 1.91:1 to 1:1. Allow for 1.91:1 ratio images if this ratio fits your interface. <br>- Retain the square image ratio for the app icon or profile image; don't alter the image. <br>- Provide enough space to display at least 20 characters of the Advertiser Name. Make sure if the title is longer than 20 characters, that you replace the additional text with an ellipsis.<br>- Your native ad is required to include the title and Call-to-Action (CTA) provided by the advertiser. It can also include advertisers' promotional text. <br>- When creating their ads, advertisers may specify certain CTA strings, such as "Install Now."<br>- The CTA text string must be displayed in its entirety on your native ad.<br>- Do not shorten or alter the CTA text. <br>- The maximum character length for CTA is 25 characters.<br>- Clearly separate the CTA text and metadata text from the advertiser's text.<br>- If the advertiser provided metadata, such as the app rating and availability or context string, include this in your native ad. | ![](https://scontent-lax7-1.xx.fbcdn.net/v/t39.2178-6/10173490_1501222856781309_2128223753_n.jpg?_nc_cat=111&ccb=1-7&_nc_sid=34156e&_nc_ohc=I5t9qrLuGeEQ7kNvwGm1YpM&_nc_oc=Adq1Kfm3syQ0T4_gIRpHd3-qDDrJrNw3mDiSf6x9ftKjK2oaGfEmvNeReIz5zI9HwF0&_nc_zt=14&_nc_ht=scontent-lax7-1.xx&_nc_gid=1yrBmLAOyQ6XDZLgOkyyMg&_nc_ss=7b289&oh=00_Af5KMCalfhkKvGLs95LLus8eWD3YanRMlv3LaT396bN0sQ&oe=6A1124EF) |
