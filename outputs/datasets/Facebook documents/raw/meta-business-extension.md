# Meta Business Extension

Meta Business Extension (MBE) is a popup-based solution that allows users to easily set up the **Meta Pixel**, **[Catalog](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/catalog)**, and **Shops** (optional).

MBE helps businesses connect their products and services with Meta and Instagram on your platform. Once connected, you can help them utilize a large suite of powerful features. MBE is recommended for our partners as it simplifies the overall integration process.
