# FAQs

Answers to frequently asked questions about the App Review process.

## General

[What if I have several apps, will they all need to be reviewed?](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_214291442592169)

The need for app review is based on the App ID level. Each individual app using those permissions or features must be submitted for review.

[Permalink](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_214291442592169)

[What does the app review process entail?](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_1764278146954858)

For more details, visit [this page](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/apps/review). The process will give you the opportunity to provide details on which permissions you need, and how they will be used. Facebook will review the use case and determine if it is permissible under our policy. After permission review, depending on the API/permission we may have additional requirements such as business verification and contract signing.

[Permalink](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_1764278146954858)

[Will test apps inherit reviewable permissions from parent apps?](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_162907774572003)

Yes, [test apps](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/apps/test-apps) will inherit reviewable permissions from parent apps.

[Permalink](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_162907774572003)

[As Facebook announces additional API's that need to go through App Review, do I need to re-submit for the entire App Review and Business Verification process all over again?](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_1161344644008122)

As new APIs become available, these will need to be requested through App Review. Business Verification however, is only required once per Business Manager entity, so Business Verification will not be required again if new permissions or APIs are required for an app.

[Permalink](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_1161344644008122)

[If there are multiple Business Manager accounts for my business, do I need to verify all Business Managers?](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_1780425985378911)

As of August 1, 2018, you only need to verify the Business Manager which the app is connected to.

[Permalink](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_1780425985378911)

[How will you verify my business?](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_189085051942039)

As part of the review process, you may be asked for business information such as the legal name of the business, address and phone number. In addition, you may be requested to provide business documentation such as utility bills, licenses, certificates of formation, or articles of incorporation.

[Permalink](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_189085051942039)

[How long will it take to review my app?](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_2016249465305394)

At the moment we are dealing with increased volume. The entire process may take up to several weeks.

- Permission review may take up to several weeks.
- Business verification should take a few days, however it will depend on the quality of documentation.

[Permalink](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_2016249465305394)

[How do I know my status throughout the process?](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_351441385383878)

You can always find the status of business verification and contracts and steps to take in the business verification panel found in the app review tab of the app dashboard. We will send you notifications throughout the process to let you know what action is needed.

[Permalink](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_351441385383878)

[If an agency or developer manages my app and ads, who needs to go through business verification?](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_269812393580126)

The app should be linked to the Business Manager for the business that ultimately owns the app and has access to the data being generated from the app. This business should be the one who goes through the business verification process.

[Permalink](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_269812393580126)

[If I have multiple apps at different tiers (for test purposes), do they all need to go through app review and business verification?](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_190887304953018)

**App review** must be performed per app. We recommend that you review your app dashboard to get the specific list of permissions that require review.

**Business Verification** is required once per Business Manager. If you choose to associate all your apps with the same Business Manager, then you will only need to go through business verification once.

[Permalink](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_190887304953018)

[I have multiple Business Manager accounts, which one should I use?](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_173786166812470)

If you already have a Business Manager account, we recommend that you connect the app to the existing Business Manager.

If there are multiple Business Manager accounts that belong to the business, we recommend determining the rationale for multiple Business Manager accounts and aligning the app with the most appropriate Business Manager. If the business has a credit line set up through the Business Manager, we recommend that you connect the app with the one that has the credit line.

[Permalink](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_173786166812470)

[Do mobile app advertisers need to go through app review in order to set up an SDK?](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_2075441639334106)

The App Review process refers to apps that require certain API permissions. You can [read about the permissions required for review here](https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2018/07/02/app-review-deadlines-approaching/). Setting up the SDK itself does not require app review. However, the SDK does enable apps to make calls to Facebook APIs, and if those APIs require review, the app will need to submit for app review.

[Permalink](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_2075441639334106)

[What should I include in the app review submission for Lead Retrieval and/or Marketing API? I'm supposed to upload a screencast but my app doesn't involve a UI (e.g. Lead Retrieval API connects on the backend).](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_999575886878370)

You can provide a screencast of your integration, or if your app does not have an end user experience, you can provide a minimum of 2 screenshots which show the settings view of your page, your CRM or Business Manager as well as provide a Page ID for a page you'll be using through these products. You can read more about this option [here](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/apps/review/server-to-server-apps).

[Permalink](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_999575886878370)

## Before You Submit

[I want to advertise my app with mobile app install ads. Do I need to submit for review?](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_452736718267943)

No, you don't need to submit for review to run mobile app install ads. You just need an app that is live in the iTunes App Store or Google Play Store. You can follow our guide to creating [mobile app installs ads](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/ads-for-apps/mobile-app-ads).

[Permalink](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_452736718267943)

[If my app is public, how do I test features and permissions for which my app has not been approved?](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_497677010687363)

To test a new feature or permission, after your app has been [reviewed](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/apps/review) and published, use the [**Create Test App** feature](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/apps/test-apps/#creating-and-managing) in the [App Dashboard](https://developers.facebook.com/apps/) to create a clone of your production app.
In the Dashboard of your production app, click the down arrow next to the app name in the upper left navigation pane and click **Create Test App**. The app clone, created with **In Development** status, allows all [app roles](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/apps/register#developer-roles) access to all features and permissions.

[Permalink](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_497677010687363)

[Do I need permissions to be re-reviewed every time I update my app?](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_1580635142260563)

No. Once you've been approved for a permission, you can use it across any version of your app on any platform.

If you expand and develop your app on a new platform, this will not need to be submitted for review. You only need to resubmit for review if you want to request a new permission - for example, when you add a new feature to your app. Changing and submitting your App Details or Open Graph actions will not affect the permissions you've been approved for.

[Permalink](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_1580635142260563)

[The app works for me, but not the reviewer. What can I do next?](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_1561225674205302)

To approve your app submission, our review team will need to log into your app and check all Facebook integrations.

If your reviewer has been unable to load or use your app, please make sure that:

- The App URL is publicly accessible and not configured as a localhost

- You've provided the username and password needed to access your development or staging site

- Your site's security certificates are up to date and not causing errors for new users

- You're able to login and use your app as a newly created test user

- The items you submitted for review are built out and working in your app


If you're rejected again for the same reason, update your **Review Instructions** or **Add Notes** section to ask your reviewer for some clarity and additional info.

[My app was rejected for 'General Issues'. What does this mean?](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_1673190292948454)

The App Review process involves loading your app on each supported platform, logging in with Facebook, and using every Facebook integration that you're requesting in review. This often results in what we call 'general issues.' These are errors or bugs relating to loading your app, logging into your app or the general functionality of your app. This means we weren't able to test the permissions you request in your submission.

Since these are issues that prevent us from reviewing your Facebook functionality, we can't comment in detail about how your app uses the Facebook functionality you've submitted for review. Because of this, we reject with 'General Issues' and provide feedback about this on each platform.

If you receive a 'General Issues' rejection, please carefully read all of the feedback. Each platform will receive individual feedback that should explain what issues were experienced in review.

[Permalink](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_1673190292948454)

[What does it mean when the review team "could not reproduce the permission"?](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_511074925741864)

During the review process, our review team follows your instructions to reproduce how permissions are used within your app. If we can't reproduce this experience - for example, because we can't follow your instructions, or we can't log into your app - then we also can't approve the submission.

To avoid this, please:

- Provide a working version of the app that uses the permission

- Make sure your instructions in the Add Notes section are clear

- Make sure that the requested login permissions personalize the user experience and comply with our principles


In particular, for the publish\_actions permission, please confirm that your app's publishing functionality is configured correctly. We need to be able to publish your app's content back to Facebook during the review process.

[Permalink](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_511074925741864)

[I was approved for permissions but I've lost access. Why?](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/app-review/AR-FAQs#faq_1534616970040065)

The following are reasons why your app has lost permissions after initial approval:




- The app is moved to a different unverified business. All previously approved permissions will be blocked.


  - If the app is then moved back to the verified business, the permissions will be unblocked.



- The app is marked as **Provide services to other businesses** but is then moved to a different business which has not been verified.
