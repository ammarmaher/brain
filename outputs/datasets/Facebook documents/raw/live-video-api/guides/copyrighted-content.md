# Copyrighted Content

## Branded Content

Branded Content is any content that features a third-party product, brand, or sponsor ("marketer"). Verified pages (pages with a blue checkmark) can share branded content through live videos. For information about incorporating branded content within a broadcast, please see our documentation on [creating branded content](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/guides/branded-content#live-video) for Live Videos.

## Copyrighted Content

Our Rights Manager tools helps you easily upload and maintain a reference library of video content to monitor and protect, including live video broadcasts. Our [Rights Management API](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/rights-manager-api) enables publishers to claim copyright ownership for live video broadcasts and manage copyright matching rules.

Additionally, your broadcast may be stopped if we detect copyright infringement. This can be prevented by ensuring all rights have been cleared for content that will be used in live video broadcasts and licensors have [whitelisted their pages](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/rights-manager-api#videocopyright). Content management technology is in place that will potentially flag, block, and remove content that is not cleared (such as a broadcast containing unlicensed background music).
