# Using Vendors to Access ThreatExchange

ThreatExchange members may use third-party vendors or service providers to access the ThreatExchange API on their behalf, consistent with the [ThreatExchange Terms and Conditions](https://www.facebook.com/legal/threatexchange_terms/) and [Meta Platform terms](https://developers.facebook.com/terms/dfc_platform_terms/#serviceprovidersandtechproviders).

Members frequently enter into additional agreements as part of sharing on ThreatExchange which may have their own requirements on this topic. If you are in a program with one member as an administrator, you can reach out to them for questions.
