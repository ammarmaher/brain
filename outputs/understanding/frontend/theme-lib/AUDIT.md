## Dim D — Accessibility (narrow — only what a token file can affect)

- **PASS — `prefers-reduced-motion` guard on the spinner.** `.falcon-icon-spin` collapses to 1ms animation/transition under `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`. `[CODE] falcon-icons.css:48-61`. This is the right reduced-motion posture for a font-icon spinner.
- 🟡 **D1 — motion keyframes are NOT reduced-motion-guarded.** The 10 `@keyframes` (`menuIn`/`drawerIn`/`drawerInRtl`/`scrimIn` + the 6 Studio `falcon-*`) and the `--animate-*`/`--duration-falcon-*` tokens have NO `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback in this file. `[CODE] :663-698, :399-406`. Drawer/menu/scrim motion plays regardless of the user's reduced-motion preference (unless a consumer guards it). The icon spinner is guarded; the global animation tokens are not. (The fix is a media-query token override or per-consumer guards — surfaced as an upgrade in DECISION.)
- **PASS — `body{color:var(--color-falcon-neutral-900)}` default-text fix.** Prevents dark-mode descendants inheriting UA black on dark canvas (contrast fix Phase-H D-NEW-3). `[CODE] :642-660`. A real a11y *improvement* baked into the SSOT.
